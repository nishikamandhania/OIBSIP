Retail Sales Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

📌 Project Overview
This project performs Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on a retail sales dataset to understand sales trends, customer demographics, product category performance, and business insights.

🎯 Objectives
- Analyze retail sales data.
- Identify monthly and quarterly sales trends.
- Understand customer purchasing behavior.
- Explore sales performance across product categories.
- Visualize insights using Python.

🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook (VS Code)

📂 Dataset
The dataset contains 1,000 retail transactions with information such as:
- Transaction ID
- Date
- Customer ID
- Gender
- Age
- Product Category
- Quantity
- Price per Unit
- Total Amount

Dataset Source: [Kaggle – Retail Sales Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/mohammadtalib786/retail-sales-dataset)

📊 Key Insights
- Total revenue was ₹456,000.
- Electronics recorded the highest category sales.
- Q4 recorded the highest quarterly revenue.
- The 46–55 age group contributed the highest sales.
- Female customers generated slightly higher sales than male customers.
- Quantity and total amount showed a positive correlation.

📈 Analysis Performed
- Data loading and inspection
- Data cleaning
- Descriptive statistics
- Monthly and quarterly sales analysis
- Product category analysis
- Gender and age group analysis
- Correlation analysis
- Data visualization

💡 Business Recommendations
- Improve sales performance during Q3.
- Focus marketing on high-performing categories.
- Use customer age-group insights for targeted campaigns.
- Plan inventory based on monthly and quarterly trends.
